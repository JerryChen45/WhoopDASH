# Privacy Policy — whoop-dash

**Last updated: August 7, 2026**

whoop-dash is a personal, self-hosted dashboard application. It is used solely
by its owner to view their own WHOOP data on their own computer.

## What data is accessed

With your authorization via WHOOP's OAuth flow, the app reads the following
from the WHOOP API: profile basics, recovery, physiological cycles (strain),
sleep, workouts, and body measurements.

## How data is stored and used

- All data is stored **locally on the owner's computer** in plain files
  restricted to the local user account. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
- Data is used only to render a personal dashboard and generate local
  on-device notifications (e.g., low recovery, workout reminders).
- OAuth tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are
  used only to call the WHOOP API over HTTPS.

## What is NOT done with your data

- No data is sold, shared, licensed, or transferred to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
- No data is used to train models or build databases beyond the local cache.

## Data retention and deletion

The local cache covers roughly the last 30 days and is overwritten on each
refresh. Deleting the app folder removes all stored data and tokens. Access
can also be revoked at any time from your WHOOP app
(App Settings → Privacy → Connected Apps) or by contacting WHOOP.
